The network, live

Four kinds of traffic share one 20 Mbps link. At switch s2 each slice passes through its own gate, and the controller re-sizes the gates every second.

Each moving dot is a burst of data. A gate's opening shows how much bandwidth that slice is allowed. Red dots falling away are packets the switch had to drop.
